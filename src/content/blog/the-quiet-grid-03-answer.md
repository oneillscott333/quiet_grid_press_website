Square **b**, row 5 and column 6, and the digit is **2**. A tip of the
cap to everyone who spotted the lonely candidate.

Here is why it was alone. That square's row already held 6, 5, 8, 3, 9,
and 7. Its column held 1, 3, 9, 5, and 4. The box added 7, 5, and 3.
Every option but 2 was gone before we even wrote the pencil marks. The
other three squares still had company, which is why they had to wait
their turn.
