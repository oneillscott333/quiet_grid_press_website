Issue 5's square b was an 8, and a tip of the cap to everyone who marked it properly. This issue is a little different. Instead of one square, you get a whole puzzle, because there are going to be people around your table this week who could use one.

## Three squares

**One.** When you are stuck, leave. Not forever; ten minutes. Refill the mug, look out a window, come back. Tired eyes skip the same square over and over, and rested eyes find the single that was sitting there the whole time. It is the least clever technique in Sudoku and one of the most reliable.

**Two.** A finished grid on the Thanksgiving table, beside the pie, with a grandchild's pencil marks in the margin and an uncle's corrections next to them. We hope your table has one this week.

**Three.** Behind the grid: before our first book took its present shape, we ran every puzzle in it through our difficulty standard and replaced the ones that did not earn their label.
