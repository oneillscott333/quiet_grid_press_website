The naked pair in Issue 7 freed a 3 in column 4, and a tip of the cap to everyone who saw the promise before the digit. This is our last issue of the year. The next one arrives on January 18, and in between, Quiet Grid Press is going to do the thing we keep recommending: stop, and rest.

## Three squares

**One.** This week, do one puzzle slowly, on purpose. No clock. Notice the order you find things in: which square came first, which technique you reached for without thinking, where you paused. Fast solving is a pleasure. Slow solving is a different pleasure, and December is the month for it.

**Two.** Candlelight is terrible reading light and perfect thinking light. Solve by the lamp; think by the candle.

**Three.** A note from the founder, so I will switch to "I." Thank you. Sixteen weeks ago this newsletter did not exist, and now it has a room full of people who write back. I read every reply. Several of you have changed how I think about the books we make next. Quiet Grid Press will be quiet from December 23 through January 10, no posts, no emails, no replies, so that I can keep Christmas the way my family keeps it and come back rested. See you on the 18th.
