Last Monday we left a row with one empty square:

```
6   7   3   2   8   1   _   9   4
```

The missing digit is **5**. Count what is there, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9,
and the only number absent is 5. A full house: eight neighbors present,
one seat left, no debate.

If you saw it in a second, good. That second is the feeling Sudoku is
built on, and every technique you will ever learn is just a more
elaborate way of arriving at it.
